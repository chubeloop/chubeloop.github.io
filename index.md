## Biography

<img class="profile-picture" src="https://github.com/chubeloop.png" alt="KyoJun Choo">

<a href="https://kr.linkedin.com/in/kyojun-choo-8047b339a">KyoJun Choo</a> is a researcher at the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/ssu-nlp/home">Natural Language Processing Lab</a>, <a href="https://ssu.ac.kr/">Soongsil University</a>. His research focuses on <b>Large Language Models (LLMs)</b>, with particular interests in <b>LLM evaluation</b>, <b>benchmark robustness and validity</b>, <b>representation-level interpretability</b>, and <b>tool / agent capabilities</b>.

He is interested in understanding what language models encode internally, whether current benchmarks faithfully measure the capabilities they claim to evaluate, and how evaluation should evolve for tool-augmented and rapidly changing language models. He also works on Korean-language evaluation, including factuality, reasoning, safety, and culturally grounded assessment.

## Selected Projects

* **Capability Representation in LLMs**  
Investigating whether external capability requirements, such as retrieval, transformation, and world-changing actions, are encoded in hidden representations before generation.

* **Reliable LLM Benchmarking**  
Studying benchmark validity, robustness, temporal effects, and leaderboard sensitivity to better understand what benchmark scores actually measure.

* **Korean LLM Evaluation**  
Developing evaluation methodologies for Korean-language understanding and generation, including reasoning, factuality, safety, and culturally grounded language use.
